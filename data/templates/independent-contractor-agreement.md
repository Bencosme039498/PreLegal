## 4. Independent Contractor Relationship

The Contractor is an independent contractor and not an employee, partner, or agent of the Client. The Contractor controls the manner and means of performing the Services, supplies its own equipment, and is solely responsible for all taxes, insurance, and benefits related to its compensation. Nothing in this Agreement entitles the Contractor to participate in any Client employee benefit plan.

## 5. Intellectual Property

All work product, deliverables, inventions, and materials created by the Contractor specifically for the Client in performing the Services (the "**Work Product**") are the sole property of the Client upon full payment. To the extent any Work Product does not qualify as a "work made for hire," the Contractor hereby assigns to the Client all right, title, and interest in the Work Product. The Contractor retains ownership of its pre-existing materials and general know-how, and grants the Client a non-exclusive, perpetual, royalty-free license to use any pre-existing materials incorporated into the Work Product.

## 6. Confidentiality

The Contractor shall keep confidential all non-public information of the Client obtained in connection with this Agreement and shall use it solely to perform the Services. This obligation survives termination of this Agreement.

## 7. Warranties

The Contractor represents that (a) it has the right to enter into this Agreement; (b) the Work Product will be original and will not infringe the rights of any third party; and (c) it will comply with all applicable laws in performing the Services.

## 8. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement for any reason upon {{termination_notice_days}} days' written notice. Either party may terminate immediately upon written notice if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure the breach within ten (10) days after notice. Upon termination, the Client shall pay the Contractor for Services performed through the termination date.

## 9. Limitation of Liability

EXCEPT FOR BREACHES OF CONFIDENTIALITY OR INFRINGEMENT OF INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY, NEITHER PARTY IS LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, OR CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES ARISING OUT OF THIS AGREEMENT.
